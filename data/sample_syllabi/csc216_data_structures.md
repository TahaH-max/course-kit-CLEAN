Northshore State University is a fictional school; this syllabus is demo data.

Course: CSC 216 - Data Structures
Instructor: Dr. Elena Park
Term: Fall 2026 (Aug 24 - Dec 18, 2026)
Meetings: Tuesday and Thursday, 9:30 AM - 10:50 AM, Science Hall 204

## Office Hours

Dr. Park holds office hours on Tuesday from 1:00 PM to 3:00 PM in Science Hall 311, or by appointment. Teaching assistant office hours are posted on the course website each week.

## Grading

- Programming assignments: 30%
- Weekly quizzes: 10%
- Midterm exam: 25%
- Final exam: 30%
- Participation: 5%

Letter grades follow the standard scale: 90% or above is an A-range grade, 80% to 89% is a B-range grade, and 70% to 79% is a C-range grade.

## Late Work Policy

Programming assignments lose 10% per day late, up to 3 days. Assignments more than 3 days late receive a zero. Each student has two free late days for the semester, which can be applied to any programming assignment without penalty.

## Collaboration Policy

You may discuss ideas with classmates, but all code you submit must be written by you. Sharing code, copying from online sources, or submitting AI-generated code without citing it is a violation of academic integrity and results in a zero on the assignment.

## Exams

Exams are closed book. You may bring one handwritten 8.5 x 11 inch note sheet to the final exam. Make-up exams are only given for documented emergencies reported within 48 hours.
