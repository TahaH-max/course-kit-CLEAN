# ENG 205: Technical Writing

Northshore State University is a fictional school; this syllabus is demo data.

Course: ENG 205 - Technical Writing
Instructor: Dr. Maya Lindqvist
Term: Fall 2026 (Aug 24 - Dec 18, 2026)
Meetings: Wednesday, 6:00 PM - 8:40 PM, Humanities 018

## Office Hours

Dr. Lindqvist holds office hours on Wednesday from 4:30 PM to 5:30 PM in Humanities 230, and answers email within two business days.

## Grading

- Instructions document: 15%
- Proposal: 15%
- Peer reviews: 10%
- Final report draft: 15%
- Final report: 30%
- Participation: 10%

## Late Work Policy

Late papers lose one letter grade per day. Peer reviews cannot be submitted late, because your classmates depend on them to revise.

## Attendance

Students may miss two class meetings without penalty. Each additional absence lowers the participation grade by 5 percentage points.

## Writing Center

The Writing Center offers free 30-minute appointments. Students who visit the Writing Center before submitting the final report draft earn 2 bonus points on that draft.
